How a fumble pays

  1. Sell $50 or more here. You get a fumble ticket.
  2. The coin graduates from its curve. Claims open.
  3. The 30-minute median price holds 10x your sell.
  4. Claim 10% of the tokens you sold, within 90 days.

Ticket rules
  • Only sells of $50 or more made through FUMPAD print a ticket. You must have held for 10 minutes.
  • Buying the same coin back within 7 days shrinks that ticket by the amount you rebought.
  • Up to 5 open tickets per coin. Creators can't claim on their own coin.
  • 10x is checked against the 30-minute median price, so one spike can't unlock a ticket.
  • If the pool is short, you join the queue in order of sell time. Queued tickets don't expire. A smaller claim that fits the pool can go first.
  • One claim can take at most 10% of the pool. This is a reward, not insurance.
