# Direction C: Signal

**Concept.** FUMPAD as a precision instrument. Everything is calm, grey and measured, so the one thing that isn't (your fumble turning into money) hits hard. The interface stays serious and the humour lives in the copy ("Sold too early. Officially.", "Later. It might go higher.").

**Palette.** One signal, International Orange `#FF4F00`, used only for 10× crossings, claims and payouts. The rest is pure black, white and cool instrument grey `#ECEDEF`. There's no red or green: losses are graphite and money is orange. `#B93A00` carries orange at text size. Dark mode is pure black.

**Type.** Archivo across its width axis: 125 wide for the wordmark and headlines, 62 condensed for coin tiles. Martian Mono covers every number, with DEX-style subscript zeros (`0.0₅94`).

**Signature moments.**
- **The 10× gauge:** a log ruler from 1× to 30×, hatched past 10×, where the fill turns orange after the crossing. It appears on every screen.
- **The unlock:** letterbox bars on a black stage. The needle sweeps and the screen cuts hard to orange at 10×. Then the title wipes in, the amount counts up and a "PAID" stamp lands. No confetti.
- **The mark:** a ring for your sell, a dotted line for the run you missed and an orange dot for 10×. The product's own chart is the logo.

**Ownable.** It has one colour that only ever means money, a gauge that becomes the brand, and a voice that's a little sorry for you.
